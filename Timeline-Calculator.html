Presence is evidence, not a guarantee. Timeline can mislabel visits and miss departures. Credited hours include only overlap with your schedule. The summary CSV has start/end columns for each time block. Use detailed intervals for totals that deduct breaks.
Supported exports & privacy

Supports Google Timeline semanticSegments, older timelineObjects/placeVisit exports, raw locations with timed coordinates, rawSignals positions, and record arrays containing visits or timelinePath points. Recognized coordinates are required. Other formats show an error rather than an invented result.

Processing runs locally with no analytics, API keys, remote fonts, or external libraries. Your browser saves settings only. Loaded history and edits disappear when you close or reload this tab. Downloads may contain sensitive attendance details.

An 80 MB JSON file also needs memory to parse and process. Performance varies by device; use a computer if your phone runs out of memory.
